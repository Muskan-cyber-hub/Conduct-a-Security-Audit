<h1>Botium Toys Internal Security Audit</h1>
<h2>Description</h2>
The objective of this internal security audit is to evaluate the security risks associated with Botium Toys’ growing online business. The audit focuses on the company’s information technology environment, business operations, customer information, online payments, and compliance responsibilities.
Botium Toys is a small U.S. toy company with one physical location that serves as its main office, storefront, and warehouse. The company has expanded its online presence and now serves customers in the United States and other countries. This growth increases the importance of protecting its information systems and business data.
